Apulza design system

Counselor suite → interactive snippets

The counselor side of the product — a live risk-score gauge (the same count-up-and-fill animation as RiskScoreGauge.tsx), the factor breakdown that explains a score, and the caseload stats bar counselors see first.

3 snippets gauge is interactive counselor-facing, not student-facing
Different audience, different tone — CLAUDE.md's compassionate-copy rules govern the student-facing product. This is internal triage tooling for counselors, so labels here stay plain and clinical ("Critical", "High Risk") rather than reframed — that directness is what the tool is for. Risk severity also needed a 4th and 5th token (--c-risk-high / --c-risk-critical) beyond DESIGN.md's base 3 semantic states — documented inline in the base CSS below.
