Apulza beta terms

Version 2026-09-30 · Back to sign-up

Thanks for helping us test Apulza. This page explains, in plain language, what joining the beta means. You must be 18 or older to take part.

It's a test version

  • Things may break, look unfinished, or change without notice.
  • We may reset beta data, including study plans and chats, as we improve the app.
  • Please don't rely on Apulza as your only way to track important deadlines during the beta.

What the app stores

  • Account info: your name, email, and role (student or counselor).
  • Study plans you create in the app.
  • Tutoring chats with the AI study helper.
  • Usage events, such as which features you open, so we can see what works.
  • School and course activity, such as attendance, activity in your school's learning system, and assignments past their due date. This is used for the student risk score described below.
  • Disabilities you choose to add to your profile. Your profile offers a list of disabilities (for example dyslexia or ADHD) that you can select if you believe they apply to you. No proof or diagnosis is needed, and you can skip this entirely.

Disability information in your profile

  • It's optional. You can skip it, and change or remove it anytime in your profile.
  • If you add it, Apulza uses it to adapt your study plans, MyTutor, and AI recommendations. For example, it may suggest a dyslexia-friendly font, or the Pomodoro technique for ADHD.
  • Counselors cannot see it.
  • It is sent to Groq, our outside AI provider, to generate these recommendations (see below).

Who can see it

  • The Apulza team, to run the beta and fix problems.
  • Counselors. During the beta, every counselor account can see a risk score for every student. The score combines several factors, such as attendance, learning-system activity, and assignments past their due date. Counselors don't see your tutoring chats or the disability information in your profile.

AI features

Apulza's AI features use an outside AI service, Groq. When you use them, your messages are sent to Groq to generate a response. If you've added disability information to your profile, it is sent too, so the recommendations can be adapted to you. Avoid sharing other private health details in chats.

About this sign-up form

Your answers on the beta sign-up form are stored separately and used only to choose and contact beta testers. Your contact details and basic answers are also copied to a Google Sheet that only the Apulza beta team can open. The sign-up form doesn't ask for diagnoses, medical history, student ID numbers, grades, or school login details.

How to leave

You can leave the beta at any time. In the app, you can export your data and delete your account yourself. To also remove your sign-up form answers, email us at the address below.

Questions

Contact the Apulza team at apulzaai@outlook.com.
